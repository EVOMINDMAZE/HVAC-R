# User Journey Guide: How the HVAC-R App Works

This guide explains the step-by-step workflows for each user role in the HVAC-R application. Use this document to understand what actions are available to you after signing up and creating a company.

## Admin/Owner Journey

As an admin who owns a company, you have the highest level of access and can perform the following actions:

### 1. Initial Setup & Company Management
- **Create your company**: After signing up as an admin, you can create your HVAC company in the app
- **Company settings**: Configure your company name, address, contact information, and branding
- **Team management**: Invite team members with different roles (manager, technician, client)

### 2. Team Building & Invitations
- **Invite managers**: Send invitation links to managers who will oversee operations
- **Invite technicians**: Add field technicians who will perform HVAC work
- **Invite clients**: Bring clients into the portal to view their assets and request service
- **Role management**: Assign appropriate permissions to each team member

### 3. Billing & Subscription Management
- **View billing dashboard**: See your current subscription plan and usage
- **Upgrade/downgrade plans**: Change your subscription tier as your business grows
- **View invoices**: Access billing history and download invoices
- **Manage payment methods**: Update credit card information

### 4. Operational Oversight
- **Dispatch overview**: Monitor job assignments and technician locations
- **Fleet management**: View technician status, vehicle locations, and key metrics
- **Reporting**: Access advanced analytics and generate business reports
- **Compliance tracking**: Ensure regulatory requirements are met

### 5. Advanced Features
- **Integration configuration**: Connect with Stripe (payments), Skool (community), and email services
- **Data import/export**: Bulk upload clients, assets, or export data for analysis
- **Audit logs**: Review user activity and system changes
- **Ownership transfer**: Transfer company ownership to another admin (if needed)

## Manager Journey

As a manager invited to a company, you can:

### 1. Team Oversight
- **View team members**: See all technicians and their current status
- **Monitor assignments**: Track which jobs are assigned to which technicians
- **Cannot invite new members**: (Permission restricted to admin/owner)

### 2. Job Management
- **Access dispatch dashboard**: View all active and pending jobs
- **Assign technicians**: Assign available technicians to unassigned jobs
- **Job approval**: Review and approve job details before dispatch

### 3. Client Management
- **View client portfolio**: See all clients and their assets
- **Client communication**: Respond to service requests and updates
- **Cannot modify client billing**: (Permission restricted to admin/owner)

### 4. Reporting & Analytics
- **Access reporting dashboards**: View key performance indicators
- **Generate basic reports**: Create operational reports for review
- **Fleet dashboard**: Monitor technician locations and activity

### 5. Restricted Areas (Cannot Access)
- Admin-only pages (`/admin`, `/billing`, `/subscription`)
- Company ownership transfer
- Subscription management
- Audit logs

## Technician Journey

As a technician in the field, you can:

### 1. Daily Operations
- **View assigned jobs**: Access your personal job board with assigned tasks
- **Job status updates**: Mark jobs as accepted, traveling, arrived, completed
- **Job details**: View client information, asset details, and work instructions

### 2. Technical Tools
- **Calculators**: Use HVAC-specific calculators (standard cycle, refrigerant comparison, DIY calculators)
- **Troubleshooting wizard**: Step-by-step diagnostic assistance
- **Reference materials**: Access technical documentation and guides

### 3. Mobile Field Work
- **Mobile-optimized interface**: Access all features on mobile devices
- **Offline mode**: Continue working without internet connection (select features)
- **Time tracking**: Log hours spent on jobs

### 4. Restricted Areas (Cannot Access)
- Admin pages (`/admin`, `/settings/team`, `/clients`)
- Manager dashboards (`/dashboard/team`, `/compliance`, `/reports/admin`)
- Billing and subscription management

## Client Journey

As a client invited to the portal, you can:

### 1. Asset Management
- **View your assets**: See all HVAC equipment associated with your account
- **Asset details**: Access maintenance history, specifications, and documentation
- **Request service**: Submit new service requests for specific assets

### 2. Service Requests
- **Submit requests**: Describe issues and request technician visits
- **Track progress**: Monitor the status of your service requests
- **View history**: Access past service records and invoices

### 3. Communication & Billing
- **Invoices**: View and download past invoices
- **Messages**: Communicate with your service provider
- **Notification preferences**: Choose how you receive updates (email/SMS)

### 4. Restricted Access
- Cannot see other clients' data
- Cannot access company management features
- Cannot view technician assignments or internal operations

## Student Journey

As a student using the learning platform, you can:

### 1. Learning Tools
- **HVAC calculators**: Practice with standard cycle, refrigerant comparison, and DIY calculators
- **Interactive tutorials**: Step through guided learning scenarios
- **Web stories**: Visual learning modules with interactive elements

### 2. Skill Development
- **Progress tracking**: Monitor your learning progress
- **Certificate generation**: Earn certificates for completed modules
- **Accessibility features**: Use screen-reader compatible interfaces

### 3. Restricted Areas
- Cannot access company-specific features
- Cannot view client data or job management
- Limited to educational content only

## Getting Started

### For Admin/Owner:
1. Sign up with admin credentials
2. Create your company profile
3. Invite your first team members (manager, technician, client)
4. Configure billing and subscription
5. Start creating jobs and managing operations

### For Manager:
1. Accept the invitation link from admin
2. Log in with provided credentials
3. Access dispatch dashboard to assign technicians
4. Monitor team performance and client requests

### For Technician:
1. Accept the invitation link from admin/manager
2. Log in and view assigned jobs
3. Use calculators and tools for field work
4. Update job status as you complete tasks

### For Client:
1. Accept the invitation link from your service provider
2. Log in to view your assets
3. Submit service requests as needed
4. Track progress and view invoices

### For Student:
1. Sign up for student access
2. Explore learning tools and calculators
3. Complete interactive tutorials
4. Track your learning progress

## Common Workflows

### Service Request Flow:
1. Client submits service request through portal
2. Manager reviews and assigns to available technician
3. Technician accepts job, travels to location, completes work
4. Technician updates job status to completed
5. Client receives notification and can view completed work details
6. Admin generates invoice for the service

### New Team Member Flow:
1. Admin generates invitation link for specific role
2. New user clicks link and creates account
3. User joins company with appropriate permissions
4. User can immediately access role-specific features

### Billing Cycle:
1. Monthly subscription automatically charges admin's payment method
2. Admin receives invoice via email and can view in app
3. Admin can upgrade/downgrade plan as needed
4. Usage metrics available in billing dashboard
